GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Can you promote my song on social media?

Promotion options can include creative posts, reels, page features and paid campaign coordination. Platforms, placements, schedule, audience and charges must be agreed before the campaign starts.

Can you create reels from my song or existing video?

Yes, provide the original audio/video and your preferred style. The quote depends on duration, number of versions, effects, subtitles and turnaround time.

Can you edit audio and video together?

Yes. Audio synchronisation, clean-up and video editing can be scoped together. Advanced mixing, mastering, recording or studio services should be confirmed separately.

Can you guarantee views, followers or viral reach?

No. Organic reach and platform distribution are not controllable. Paid advertising can target agreed audiences, but outcomes vary and are not guaranteed.

Will you handle copyright for any song?

We can help organise permission requests, licence details, credits and platform processes. We cannot transfer ownership, grant rights we do not control or guarantee that a copyright claim will be removed. The client must have the necessary rights or written permission.

Are advertising and collaboration fees included?

Not automatically. Ad spend, influencer fees, page placement charges and third-party costs are itemised separately in the written quote unless explicitly included.

How do I start?

Send a WhatsApp message with your service requirement, links, references, deadline and budget range. The team can confirm feasibility, deliverables and a quote.
